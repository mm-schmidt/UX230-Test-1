Tim Berners-Lee

Tim Berners-Lee is an English computer scientist, known for inventing the World Wide Web. In 1989, he proposed a digital information management system. He implemented the first successful Internet communication between an HTTP client and server later that year, releasing the World Wide Web into the public domain in 1993.

Berners-Lee created the World Wide Web to be universally adopted, shared, and built upon by billions of people, and is responsible for one of the greatest inventions of the 20th century.
